# hyprmoncfg panel 2.6.0

The panel has a new look. Your displays sit on a stage as lit screens, the panel sizes itself to what it shows instead of leaving empty space, and every setting uses a control that fits it. It works with [hyprmoncfg 1.19.0](https://github.com/crmne/hyprmoncfg/releases/tag/v1.19.0) or newer. The lid-closed match reasons need [hyprmoncfg 1.22.0](https://github.com/crmne/hyprmoncfg/releases/tag/v1.22.0), which brings the same redesign to the TUI.

## New

- **Displays are screens on a stage.** Each display is drawn as a lit screen with its connector and model, its workspaces as chips, and its mode, scale and position. The compact view now leads with the layout.
- **The panel fits its content.** It used to open at a fixed 1120x812 whatever it showed. The Layout page now takes its size from your arrangement, and Workspaces and Profiles grow with their rows, then scroll. It never grows past your screen, and it never resizes while you drag a display.
- **Profiles show their layout.** Each saved profile has a small picture of its arrangement next to its name and match score.
- **Controls that follow Omarchy's own Display panel.**
  - Scale is a row of pills, as in Omarchy's panel: 1x, 1.25x, 1.5x, 1.6x, 2x and 3x, each adjusted to the nearest sharp scale above it for that display, with **More** listing every sharp scale. A scale you set yourself keeps its own pill and is never changed.
  - Choice rows use Omarchy's own button group, so they look and respond like the rest of the shell.
  - Position X and Y are exact pixel fields. Dragging, arrow keys and snapping stay the main way to move a display.
  - **Place beside** puts the selected display Left, Right, Above or Below its nearest neighbour, using the same snapping as Alt+arrows.
  - VRR, colour depth, SDR EOTF and the WCG and HDR overrides show every choice in a row. Rotation separates the angle from **Flipped**.
  - A value the panel doesn't recognise stays visible as its own choice and is never rewritten.
- **Keep or revert, at a glance.** The confirmation after a preview shows a draining countdown and a small picture of the layout you're about to keep.
- **The Color tab grows the panel** instead of scrolling inside it.

## Fixed

- **"Automatically use the best profile" can be turned off without an exact match** ([#20](https://github.com/crmne/omarchy-hyprmoncfg/issues/20)). The toggle stayed disabled, with no reason given, whenever no saved profile was exactly on screen, which is the usual state after closing the lid while docked. Turning it off now previews the recommended profile, and keeping it pins that profile. With nothing to recommend, it says to save a profile for these displays. Reported by @brdweb.
- **The score breakdown names the lid-closed reasons** from hyprmoncfg 1.22.0: "built-in, kept off with the lid closed" and "built-in, turned off by the closed lid".
- **The last dropdown in the Display tab is no longer clipped,** and fields in the right-hand column keep their full border.

## Known limitations

- **A layout that already overlaps,** for example one saved that way, can't be edited until the overlap is fixed. The TUI lets you edit it.
- **Resizing waits for the pointer to leave the panel** when its width changes or the bar isn't at the top, so the control under your pointer never moves.
- **Long positions are shortened** on profile cards in narrow panels.

## What's tested

Node tests (161), offscreen QML tests (21), Quickshell checks of the new controls and plugin validation pass. The scale presets are checked against the same test cases as the TUI. The screenshots were rendered offscreen with sample data. The redesign was used on the maintainer's desktop; it hasn't been tried with a bottom or side bar, fractional scaling or larger fonts.
