# hyprmoncfg panel 2.5.0

The panel now shows which displays aren't working as saved: one that's on but shows nothing, and one hyprmoncfg runs below its saved settings. It works with [hyprmoncfg 1.19.0](https://github.com/crmne/hyprmoncfg/releases/tag/v1.19.0) or newer. The note about a stepped-down display needs [hyprmoncfg 1.21.0](https://github.com/crmne/hyprmoncfg/releases/tag/v1.21.0).

![The layout editor with a display that has no usable signal and one running below its saved settings](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/crmne/omarchy-hyprmoncfg/v2.5.0/design/releases/2.5.0-display-health.png)

*Synthetic displays, rendered offscreen.*

## New

- **Displays that are on but show nothing get their own row.** They used to appear as a normal card at their saved position, as if they worked. They now get a row marked **No usable signal**, next to displays that are off or mirrored, and the Monitor Layout summary names them too.
- **A display running below its saved settings says so.** When hyprmoncfg steps a display down because it wouldn't stay on at its saved settings, its card shows how it runs, for example *Running at 120 Hz without VRR*. Your saved profile is unchanged.

## Known limitations

- **With hyprmoncfg older than 1.21.0,** the panel still shows "No usable signal", but not the stepped-down note.
- **"No usable signal" doesn't yet say whether recovery is in progress.**

## What's tested

Node tests, offscreen QML tests and plugin validation pass. The screenshot was rendered offscreen with sample data, not captured on a running desktop.
