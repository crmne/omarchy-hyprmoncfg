# hyprmoncfg panel 2.4.1

Turn workspace rules off from the Strategy menu. It works with [hyprmoncfg 1.19.0](https://github.com/crmne/hyprmoncfg/releases/tag/v1.19.0) or newer; [1.19.1](https://github.com/crmne/hyprmoncfg/releases/tag/v1.19.1) also fixes a flicker after closing the lid while docked.

## Workspace Strategy has an Off choice

If another tool manages your workspaces, such as hyprsplit, set **Strategy** to **Off** on the Workspaces page ([hyprmoncfg#70](https://github.com/crmne/hyprmoncfg/issues/70)). hyprmoncfg then writes no workspace rules for that profile.

- Strategy now lists Off, Manual, Sequential, Interleaved, same as the TUI. The separate Enabled toggle is gone.
- Off keeps your plan. Pick a strategy again and your workspace count, group size and monitor order are still there.
- While Off, the plan settings and monitor order are hidden and the plan reads "Off: hyprmoncfg writes no workspace rules."
- Profiles that already had workspace rules turned off open as Off. Nothing in your saved files changes.
- Opening a profile that's Off no longer marks it as edited.

## What's tested

Node tests, offscreen QML tests and plugin validation pass. The screenshots of Off were rendered offscreen with sample data, not captured on a running desktop.
